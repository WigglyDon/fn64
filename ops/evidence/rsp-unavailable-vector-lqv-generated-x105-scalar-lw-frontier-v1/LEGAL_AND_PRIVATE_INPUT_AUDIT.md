# Legal And Private-Input Audit

The product and proofs use only public generated cold-x105 inputs, the pinned
public N64-IPL source, the public RSP Programmer's Guide, and independent
synthetic byte patterns.

No private PIF, commercial cartridge, user-task bytes, user-derived RSP word,
private path, basename, hash, microcode-family recognition, title policy, or
digest policy was read or embedded. The complete public RSP program is not
copied here. The result earns neither BOOT-3 nor a compatibility claim.
